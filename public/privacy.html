🌤️ Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

SunScout does not collect any personal information. We do not require login, registration, or any personal details.

Local storage

Your saved "My List" favorites are stored only on your device using browser localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers.

Weather data

We use the Open-Meteo API to show local weather. This requires sending your selected city name — no personal data is shared.

Google Maps

Activity photos are fetched via Google Places API. No personal data is sent to Google through our app.

No tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking tools. We do not sell or share any data.

Children's privacy

SunScout is designed for families. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.
