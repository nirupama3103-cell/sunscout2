Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

If you subscribe to our email list, we store your email address to send you weekend activity picks. Nothing else. You can unsubscribe any time using the link in every email.

What we don’t collect

We don’t ask for your name, your children’s information, or your precise location. City selection stays on your device.

Local storage only

Your saved activities (My List) and notes are stored on your device and never sent to any server.

Analytics

We use a privacy-focused analytics tool to count page views and understand which activities are popular. This is aggregate only — we don’t build profiles or track individuals across sites.

Sponsors

Some listings may be marked as sponsored or featured. These are clearly labelled. Sponsors never receive your personal data.

Children’s privacy

SunScout is built for families. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions? Email sunscout2026@gmail.com.
