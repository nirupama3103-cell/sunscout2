Four things worth knowing before you start

  • Cover the table first. Paint, glue and slime all travel. An old sheet or newspaper saves the cleanup.
  • Check the supply list. Every project lists what it needs. Most of it is already in a kitchen drawer.
  • Adult steps are marked. Irons, scissors and hot glue are called out in the steps. Read those first.
  • Keep the scraps. Leftover beads, card and yarn become the next project.
