🌈 Rainbow Fuse-Bead Butterfly & Star

Ages 5+ 30–45 min Low mess Low cost

What You'll Need

  • Fuse beads (red, blue, and other favorite colors)
  • Plastic pegboards (butterfly, star, or heart shapes)
  • Parchment paper or ironing paper (never wax paper!)
  • A household iron (adult-supervised)
  • Optional: tweezers for placing tiny beads

📝 Detailed Instructions

  1. Set up your station: Use a flat, stable table. Place one pegboard in front of you.
  2. Place beads one by one: For a butterfly, outline the wings in dark blue, then fill with bright red. For a star or heart, work symmetrically from center. Beads should sit loosely on the pegs—don't press down hard.
  3. Prep for ironing: Carefully move the pegboard to your ironing station. Turn off steam and set the iron to medium dry heat (wool setting works best).
  4. Fuse (adults only): Place parchment paper flat over your design. Iron slowly in small circles for 10–20 seconds. You'll see colors become vibrant as the bead tops melt together. Don't press hard.
  5. Cool & finish: Let it cool 1 minute. Peel off parchment paper. Flip it over, re-apply parchment, and iron the back side for 10 seconds. Place a heavy book on top for 5 minutes while cooling completely.
⚠️ Safety note: The iron gets very hot—adult supervision required for steps 3 and 4. Fuse beads are small and not recommended for children under 3. This is a great project for kids 5+ with parental help.
