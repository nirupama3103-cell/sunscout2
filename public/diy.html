Fuse BeadsAges 3+

Place tiny colorful beads onto a pegboard to make a design, then fuse them together with a warm iron to create a keepsake you can hold, hang, or gift!

💡 What Kids Learn
  • Fine motor & color sorting
  • Symmetry & spatial reasoning
  • Patience & creative expression
📦 You'll Need
  • 5mm fuse beads + pegboard
  • Ironing paper or parchment
  • Household iron (adult only)
📋 Steps
  1. Arrange beads on pegboard into your pattern
  2. Lay ironing paper flat over beads
  3. Adult irons on medium, slow circles 10–20s
  4. Cool, peel paper, lift your creation!

LEGO BuildingAges 2+

Stack bricks to build anything you can imagine — a cozy house, a tall tower, or a whole city! DUPLO for little hands, classic LEGO for bigger builders.

Snakes & LaddersAges 3+

Race to space 100 on a numbered grid — climb ladders to zoom ahead, or slide down snakes and try again! A classic game of luck, counting, and never giving up.

💡 What Kids Learn
  • Number recognition & counting
  • Turn-taking & social skills
  • Basic addition & resilience
📦 You'll Need
  • Snakes & Ladders board
  • One die · Game tokens · 2–4 players
📋 How to Play
  1. Each player starts token on space 1
  2. Roll die and move forward that many spaces
  3. Climb ladders, slide down snakes
  4. First to reach space 100 wins!

✨
Chinese CheckersAges 6+

Race your marbles across a star-shaped board to the opposite corner before your opponents do. Hop over other marbles to leap ahead — strategy wins here!

💡 What Kids Learn
  • Strategic thinking & planning ahead
  • Pattern recognition & spatial logic
  • Competitive & cooperative play
📦 You'll Need
  • Star-shaped Chinese Checkers board
  • 60 marbles in 6 colors · 2–6 players
📋 How to Play
  1. Place 10 marbles in one triangle corner
  2. Move one marble per turn
  3. Step to adjacent spot or jump over marbles
  4. First to fill the opposite triangle wins!

🤔
Marble SolitaireAges 8+

A solo brain-teaser! Start with a full board and one empty center hole. Jump marbles over each other to remove them — your goal is to leave just one marble standing, right in the middle.

💡 What Kids Learn
  • Logical deduction & puzzle-solving
  • Working memory & reverse thinking
  • Patience & persistence
📦 You'll Need
  • Cross-shaped board (33 holes)
  • 32 marbles or pegs · 1 player
📋 How to Play
  1. Fill all holes with marbles except the center
  2. Hop a marble over an adjacent one into empty hole
  3. Remove the jumped marble each time
  4. Win: only 1 marble left in the center!

Homemade Slime Lab
Ages 4+

Mix glue, baking soda & contact solution to make stretchy, squishy slime in any color you want!

🧪
🧪
Homemade Slime LabAges 4+

Mix glue, baking soda, and contact lens solution to create stretchy, squishy slime in any color you choose — a classic STEM sensory experiment kids beg to do again!

💡 What Kids Learn
  • Basic chemistry & states of matter
  • Measuring & following instructions
  • Sensory exploration & creativity
📦 You'll Need
  • White PVA school glue · Baking soda
  • Contact lens solution (must contain boric acid)
  • Food coloring · Mixing bowl & spoon
📋 Steps
  1. Pour ½ cup glue into bowl, add food coloring
  2. Mix in ½ tsp baking soda until smooth
  3. Add 1 tbsp contact solution, stir fast
  4. Knead until slime forms — stretch & play!

Nature Terrarium

🌿
🌿
Nature TerrariumAges 4+

Build a tiny living world inside a glass jar using rocks, soil, and small plants gathered from your Bay Area backyard or local park — a mini ecosystem that kids water and watch grow!

💡 What Kids Learn
  • Plant biology & ecosystems
  • Responsibility through daily care
  • Nature observation & patience
📦 You'll Need
  • Glass jar or clear container (wide-mouth)
  • Pebbles · Activated charcoal (optional) · Potting soil
  • Small plants: succulents, moss, or ferns
  • Decorative rocks, mini figurines
📋 Steps
  1. Add 1″ pebble layer for drainage
  2. Sprinkle thin layer of charcoal to keep it fresh
  3. Add 2–3″ of potting soil, shape hills & valleys
  4. Plant greenery, decorate, mist lightly & enjoy!

Paper Circuit Cards

💡
Paper Circuit CardsAges 8+

Use copper tape and a tiny LED to make greeting cards that actually light up — a Silicon Valley-inspired STEM craft that teaches real electrical circuits through art!

💡 What Kids Learn
  • Basic electrical circuits (series & parallel)
  • Conductors, insulators & polarity
  • Engineering design & creative problem-solving
📦 You'll Need
  • Cardstock or thick paper · Copper tape
  • 3V coin battery (CR2032) · LED lights
  • Markers, stickers for decorating
📋 Steps
  1. Draw your card design, mark where LED will shine
  2. Lay copper tape in a loop from + to – battery spot
  3. Place LED legs across the tape gap (match + / –)
  4. Slide battery in, fold card — watch it glow! 🌟
