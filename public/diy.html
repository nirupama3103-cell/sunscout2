Fuse Beads
Ages 3+

Place tiny colorful beads onto a pegboard to make a design, then fuse them together with a warm iron to create a keepsake you can hold, hang, or gift!

💡 What Kids Learn
  • Fine motor & color sorting
  • Symmetry & spatial reasoning
  • Patience & creative expression
📦 You'll Need
  • 5mm fuse beads + pegboard
  • Ironing paper or parchment
  • Household iron (adult only)
📋 Steps
  1. Arrange beads on pegboard into your pattern
  2. Lay ironing paper flat over beads
  3. Adult irons on medium, slow circles 10–20s
  4. Cool, peel paper, lift your creation!

🏠
LEGO Building
Ages 2+

Stack bricks to build anything you can imagine — a cozy house, a tall tower, or a whole city! DUPLO for little hands, classic LEGO for bigger builders.

💡 What Kids Learn
  • Spatial engineering & balance
  • 3D visualization & planning
  • Creative problem solving
📦 You'll Need
  • Ages 2–8: DUPLO baseplates & large blocks
  • Ages 8+: Standard LEGO bricks, roof tiles
  • Flat sturdy surface
📋 Steps
  1. Place a flat baseplate on a level surface
  2. Arrange one layer to outline walls
  3. Stack bricks upward, stagger joints
  4. Bridge the top with long plates or roof bricks

Snakes & Ladders
Ages 3+

Race to space 100 on a numbered grid — climb ladders to zoom ahead, or slide down snakes and try again! A classic game of luck, counting, and never giving up.

💡 What Kids Learn
  • Number recognition & counting
  • Turn-taking & social skills
  • Basic addition & resilience
📦 You'll Need
  • Snakes & Ladders board
  • One die · Game tokens · 2–4 players
📋 How to Play
  1. Each player starts token on space 1
  2. Roll die and move forward that many spaces
  3. Climb ladders, slide down snakes
  4. First to reach space 100 wins!

Chinese Checkers
Ages 6+

Race your marbles across a star-shaped board to the opposite corner before your opponents do. Hop over other marbles to leap ahead — strategy wins here!

💡 What Kids Learn
  • Strategic thinking & planning ahead
  • Pattern recognition & spatial logic
  • Competitive & cooperative play
📦 You'll Need
  • Star-shaped Chinese Checkers board
  • 60 marbles in 6 colors · 2–6 players
📋 How to Play
  1. Place 10 marbles in one triangle corner
  2. Move one marble per turn
  3. Step to adjacent spot or jump over marbles
  4. First to fill the opposite triangle wins!

Marble Solitaire
Ages 8+

A solo brain-teaser! Start with a full board and one empty center hole. Jump marbles over each other to remove them — your goal is to leave just one marble standing, right in the middle.

💡 What Kids Learn
  • Logical deduction & puzzle-solving
  • Working memory & reverse thinking
  • Patience & persistence
📦 You'll Need
  • Cross-shaped board (33 holes)
  • 32 marbles or pegs · 1 player
📋 How to Play
  1. Fill all holes with marbles except the center
  2. Hop a marble over an adjacent one into empty hole
  3. Remove the jumped marble each time
  4. Win: only 1 marble left in the center!
