Privacy Policy

What we collect. If you subscribe to our email list, we store your email address to send you weekend activity picks. Nothing else. You can unsubscribe any time using the link in every email.

What we don’t collect. We don’t ask for your name, your children’s information, or your precise location. City selection stays on your device.

Local storage only. Your saved activities (My List) and notes are stored on your device and never sent to any server.

Analytics. We use a privacy-focused analytics tool to count page views and understand which activities are popular. This is aggregate only — we don’t build profiles or track individuals across sites.

Sponsors. Some listings may be marked as sponsored or featured. These are clearly labelled. Sponsors never receive your personal data.
