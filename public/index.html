About SunScout

Bay Area Family Activity Finder

I built SunScout as a mom who knows exactly how overwhelming it is to find the right summer activities for kids. I wanted one simple spot for Bay Area families — free parks, splash pads, museums, STEM camps, and weekend adventures — all filtered by age and weather.

Built with curiosity, love, and a bit of prompt engineering. 💛

🔄 Live Data — Activities refresh from Google Places every 10 minutes
🌡️ Weather Smart — Auto-recommends indoor or outdoor based on temperature
👶 Age Filtered — From infants to teens, every activity is tagged
🗺️ Maps Ready — Every listing links directly to Google Maps
