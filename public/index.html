Privacy Policy

No tracking. SunScout does not use cookies, trackers, or analytics to monitor your behavior.

No ads. We are not ad-supported. No advertiser has access to your data.

No data collection. We do not collect your name, email, location, or any personal information.

Local storage only. Your saved activities (My List) and notes are stored only on your device and never sent to any server.

Third-party APIs. Activity data is fetched from Google Places, Eventbrite, and Ticketmaster. Their own privacy policies apply to those services.
